## Graphic designer
-**Graphic Designer** is a professional who uses visuals like *images*,*colors*,*typography* & *layout* to communicate ideas and messages clearly and creatively.

-He or she turns ideas into visual content that people see and understand easily.

<h3>What Does a Graphic Designer Do.</h3>
   1. Works on packaging designs.<br>
   2. Creates logos and brand identities.<br>
   3. Designs posters,flyers and bronchures.<br>
   4. Builds Social Media graphics.<br>
   5. Designs Websites and Application interface.<br>
   6. Creates advertisements.<br>

<h3>Tools Used by Graphic Designers.</h3>
   1. Adobe Photoshop-It is basically used for editing photos.<br>
   2. Adobe Illustrator-It is used for ligod and illustrations.<br>
   3. Canva-This tool is mostly used by beginners in graphic design.<br>
   4. Figma-It is used for UI/UX and Web design.<br>
   5. Adobe InDesign

<h3>Importance of Graphic Design.</h3>   

- It builds brand identity.
- It  attracts customers to a certain business or web page.
- It helps communication become faster and clearer.

<h3>Why is it advisable to know about Graphic Design.</h3>

1. <h4>It makes your work look professional.</h4>
-When building a **website**,**portfolio** or a **business page**, a good design attracts attention and builds trust among the users.A poorly designed website can make the users to leave quickly.

2. <h4>It improves your Web Development Skills.</h4>
-Graphic design helps you understand

+ Color combinations
+ Font pairing
+ Layout and Spacing
+ Visual Hierarchy

3. <h4>It increases Job Opportunities.</h4>
-Many companies worldWide want developers who also understand the concepts concerning designs.You have to know the various tools which gives you an advantage over the other beginners.

4. <h4>It builds creativity.</h4>
-Graphic design trains your

+ Attention to details
+ Problem-Solving skills
+ Creative thinking
+ Persistence
+ Communication
+ Research skills
+ Analytical skills
